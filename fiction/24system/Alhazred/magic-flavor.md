## Supernatural Interactions

### Fae Magic

Fae magic behaves like a projected field with a directional polarity. It may carry many different magical effects, including **Illusion** and **Enchantment**.

**Iron strongly interacts with Fae magic.** It absorbs and redirects the magical field along the shape of the iron, somewhat like magnetic field lines.

* **Straight iron** tends to channel Fae magic along its length.
* **Bent iron** redirects the field at the bend.
* **Closed iron loops** scatter or circulate Fae magic.
* Iron passing through a **Fae illusion** causes the illusion to distort, scatter, or become translucent near the iron.
* Iron worn around the head, such as a circlet, substantially increases the DC of **Fae mind-affecting magic**. Commands may arrive only as scattered words, emotions, or incomplete thoughts.
* Long iron objects pointed toward a Fae may instead provide a conduit back toward the wielder and can grant the Fae **Advantage** on appropriate magical attacks.

The traditional belief in **cold iron** is folklore rather than a separate material property. Shape, orientation, and arrangement matter more than temperature.

### Sonic Magic

Magic transmitted through sound must compete with other auditory signals.

**Competing noise increases the DC** of sonic effects. Louder, closer, or more chaotic competing sounds provide greater protection.

Blocking the sound entirely prevents effects that require the target to hear them.

### Charm

Charm effects that manipulate attention, desire, or thought are vulnerable to powerful competing sensory stimuli.

Strong odors associated with immediate biological responses—particularly **hunger and appetite**—increase the DC of Charm effects.

The stronger and fresher the odor, the greater the protection.

**Example:** Freshly crushed garlic provides greater protection than an intact garlic clove.

### Lycanthropy and Silver

Werewolves cannot maintain a human form while in direct contact with silver.

Silver does **not** inherently cause additional physical damage. Instead, contact forces the werewolf's true form to manifest.

Polished silver may therefore be used to expose a suspected werewolf without requiring a conventional attack.
