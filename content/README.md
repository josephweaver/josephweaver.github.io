# Notebook

I organize my scientific explorations, collected notes, creative work, daily activity, and thoughts into five sections.

- [Research](research/README.md): I explore scientific questions and ideas that may lead to publishable material.
- [Notes](notes/README.md): I collect notes from sources, classes, books, and papers on statistics, mathematics, agriculture, and other subjects that interest me.
- [Fiction](fiction/README.md): I develop literary work, role-playing games, and computer-game designs.
- [Worklog](worklog/README.md): I record what I did each day so I can track what went where, find the relevant work, and pick it up again.
- [Posts](posts/README.md): I write down my observations and thoughts.
