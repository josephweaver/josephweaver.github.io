# 1. Counting Traces and Sauer's Lemma - van der Vaart & Wellner §2.6.1, Lemma 2.6.2 and Corollary 2.6.3, pp. 135-136

## Professor Wrote (verbatim)

### Chapter 2.6. VC Classes

$$
V(\mathcal C)=\inf\left\{n:\max_{x_1,\ldots,x_n}\Delta_n(\mathcal C,x_1,\ldots,x_n)<2^n\right\}
$$

is the VC index.

**Goal:** Show that covering numbers of VC classes grow polynomially in $1/\varepsilon$ as $\varepsilon\to0$.

**Lemma.** Let $\{x_1,\ldots,x_n\}$ be arbitrary points. The total number of subsets $\Delta_n(\mathcal C,x_1,\ldots,x_n)$ picked out by $\mathcal C$ is bounded above by the number of subsets of $\{x_1,\ldots,x_n\}$ shattered by $\mathcal C$.

**Corollary (Sauer's lemma).** For a VC class of sets $\mathcal C$,

$$
\max_{x_1,\ldots,x_n}\Delta_n(\mathcal C,x_1,\ldots,x_n)
\leq\sum_{j=0}^{V(\mathcal C)-1}\binom nj
=O\bigl(n^{V(\mathcal C)-1}\bigr)
\qquad(n\to\infty).
$$

# 2. Covering Numbers of VC Classes of Sets - van der Vaart & Wellner, Theorem 2.6.4, p. 136

## Professor Wrote (verbatim)

**Theorem.** There is a universal constant $K$ such that, for every VC class of sets $\mathcal C$, every probability measure $Q$, every $r\geq1$, and $0<\varepsilon<1$,

$$
N(\varepsilon,\mathcal C,L_r(Q))
\leq K V(\mathcal C)(4e)^{V(\mathcal C)}
\left(\frac1\varepsilon\right)^{r(V(\mathcal C)-1)}.
$$

**Example 4.** For $S=\mathbb R^d$ and $\mathcal H$ the class of all half-spaces,

$$
V(\mathcal H)=d+2,
$$

so

$$
N(\varepsilon,\mathcal H,L_r(Q))
\leq K(d+2)(4e)^{d+2}
\left(\frac1\varepsilon\right)^{r(d+1)}.
$$

$$
\mathcal F=\{I_H:H\in\mathcal H\}.
$$

“Too simple.”

# 3. VC-Subgraph Classes and Their Covering Numbers - van der Vaart & Wellner §2.6.2, Theorem 2.6.7, p. 141

## Professor Wrote (verbatim)

**Definition.** The subgraph of a function $f:\mathcal X\to\mathbb R$ is a subset of $\mathcal X\times\mathbb R$ given by

$$
\{(x,t):t<f(x)\}.
$$

**Definition.** A collection $\mathcal F$ of measurable functions is called a VC-subgraph class if the collection of all subgraphs of $f\in\mathcal F$ is a VC class of sets in $\mathcal X\times\mathbb R$. If this subgraph class is $\mathcal C$,

$$
V(\mathcal F):=V(\mathcal C).
$$

**Theorem.** For a VC-subgraph class of functions $\mathcal F$ with measurable envelope function $F$ and $r\geq1$, for any probability measure $Q$ with $\|F\|_{L_r(Q)}>0$,

$$
N\bigl(\varepsilon\|F\|_{L_r(Q)},\mathcal F,L_r(Q)\bigr)
\leq K V(\mathcal F)(16e)^{V(\mathcal F)}
\left(\frac1\varepsilon\right)^{r(V(\mathcal F)-1)},
\qquad0<\varepsilon<1,
$$

where $K$ is a universal constant.

# 4. The Donsker Property and the Entropy Integral - van der Vaart & Wellner, Theorems 2.5.2 and 2.6.8, pp. 127 and 142

## Professor Wrote (verbatim)

**Theorem.** Let $\mathcal F$ be a pointwise separable, $P$-pre-Gaussian VC-subgraph class with envelope $F$ satisfying

$$
P^\star (F>x)=o(x^{-2})\qquad(x\to\infty).
$$

Then $\mathcal F$ is $P$-Donsker.

**Remark in proof.** The key question is

$$
\int_0^1\sqrt{\log N}\,d\varepsilon<\infty.
$$

From the preceding polynomial bound, with $V=V(\mathcal F)$, the relevant integral is bounded by a constant multiple of

$$
\int_0^1\sqrt{C-r(V-1)\log\varepsilon}\,d\varepsilon.
$$

Put $u=C-r(V-1)\log\varepsilon$. Then

$$
\varepsilon=e^{(C-u)/(r(V-1))},\qquad
 d\varepsilon=-\frac1{r(V-1)}e^{(C-u)/(r(V-1))}\,du.
$$

After this change of variable and a rescaling,

$$
\int_C^\infty\sqrt u\,e^{-(u-C)/(r(V-1))}\,du
\leq\widetilde C\int_0^\infty\sqrt v\,e^{-v}\,dv
=\widetilde C\,\Gamma\left(\frac32\right)<\infty.
$$

# 5. Convex Hulls and VC-Hull Classes - van der Vaart & Wellner §2.6.3, Theorem 2.6.9, pp. 142-144

## Professor Wrote (verbatim)

**Definition.** The symmetric convex hull of a class $\mathcal F$, denoted by $\operatorname{sconv}(\mathcal F)$, is

$$
\left\{\sum_{i=1}^m a_i f_i:
\sum_{i=1}^m|a_i|\leq1,\ f_i\in\mathcal F,\ m\geq1\right\}.
$$

**Definition.** A set $\mathcal F$ of measurable functions is called a VC-hull class if there is a VC-subgraph class of functions $\mathcal G$ such that every $f\in\mathcal F$ is the pointwise limit of a sequence

$$
f_m\in\operatorname{sconv}(\mathcal G).
$$

**Theorem.** Let $Q$ be a probability measure on $(\mathcal X,\mathcal A)$. Let $\mathcal F$ be a class of measurable functions with measurable envelope $F$ such that $QF^2<\infty$ and

$$
N\bigl(\varepsilon\|F\|_{L_2(Q)},\mathcal F,L_2(Q)\bigr)
\leq C\left(\frac1\varepsilon\right)^V,
\qquad0<\varepsilon<1.
$$

There exists a constant $K$, depending only on $C$ and $V$, such that

$$
\log N\bigl(\varepsilon\|F\|_{L_2(Q)},
\overline{\operatorname{conv}}\mathcal F,L_2(Q)\bigr)
\leq K\left(\frac1\varepsilon\right)^{2V/(V+2)}.
$$

The closure contains pointwise limits.

# 6. Entropy of VC-Hull Classes - van der Vaart & Wellner, Corollary 2.6.12, p. 145

## Professor Wrote (verbatim)

**Corollary.** For any VC-hull class $\mathcal F$ and probability measure $Q$,

$$
\log N\bigl(\varepsilon\|F\|_{L_2(Q)},\mathcal F,L_2(Q)\bigr)
\leq K\left(\frac1\varepsilon\right)^{2-2/V_m(\mathcal F)},
$$

where $K$ depends only on $V_m(\mathcal F)$.

Writing $V=V_m(\mathcal F)$,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_0^1\sqrt{\log N}\,d\varepsilon
&\leq\sqrt K\int_0^1\varepsilon^{-1+1/V}\,d\varepsilon\\
&=\sqrt K\left.\frac{\varepsilon^{1/V}}{1/V}\right|_0^1
=\sqrt K\,V<\infty.
\end{aligned}
$$

# 7. VC-Major Classes - van der Vaart & Wellner §2.6.4, Lemma 2.6.13 and Theorem 2.6.14, pp. 145-146

## Professor Wrote (verbatim)

**Alternative definition.** A class $\mathcal F$ of measurable functions is called a VC-major class if the sets

$$
\bigl\{\{x:f(x)>t\}:f\in\mathcal F,\ t\in\mathbb R\bigr\}
$$

form a VC class of sets.

**Lemma.** A bounded VC-major class is a scalar multiple of a VC-hull class for sets.

**Idea of proof.** For a function $f:\mathcal X\to[0,1]$, define

$$
f_m(x)=\frac1m\sum_{i=1}^m I\left\{f(x)>\frac im\right\}.
$$

Then

$$
\mathcal F\subseteq
\overline{\operatorname{conv}}
\bigl\{I_{\{f>t\}}:f\in\mathcal F,\ t\in\mathbb R\bigr\},
$$

where the closure is pointwise sequential closure.

**Theorem.** Let $\mathcal F$ be a pointwise separable VC-major class with measurable envelope $F$ such that

$$
\int_0^\infty\sqrt{P^\star (F>x)}\,dx<\infty.
$$

Then $\mathcal F$ is $P$-Donsker.

VC-subgraph, VC-major, VC-hull $\longrightarrow$ Donsker, under the stated conditions. Bypass a direct weak-convergence argument.
