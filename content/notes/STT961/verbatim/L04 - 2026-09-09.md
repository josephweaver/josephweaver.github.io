# 1. Bounded Functions and Stochastic Processes - van der Vaart & Wellner §1.5, p. 34

## Professor Wrote (verbatim)

### Ch. 1.5. Spaces of Bounded Functions

For an index set $T$,

$$
\ell^\infty(T)=\left\{z:T\to\mathbb R:\|z\|_T=\sup_{t\in T}|z(t)|<\infty\right\}.
$$

$$
\{X(t):t\in T\}\quad\text{is a stochastic process.}
$$

If $t\mapsto X(t,\omega)$ is bounded, then

$$
X:\Omega\to\ell^\infty(T).
$$

# 2. Asymptotic Measurability from Coordinates - van der Vaart & Wellner §1.5, Lemma 1.5.2, p. 35

## Professor Wrote (verbatim)

### Weak Convergence in $\ell^\infty(T)$

**1.** Let $X_\alpha:\Omega_\alpha\to\ell^\infty(T)$ be asymptotically tight. Then it is asymptotically measurable if and only if $X_\alpha(t)$ is asymptotically measurable for every $t\in T$.

# 3. Marginals and Weak Convergence - van der Vaart & Wellner §1.5, Lemma 1.5.3 and Theorem 1.5.4, p. 35

## Professor Wrote (verbatim)

**2.** Let $X$ and $Y$ be tight Borel measurable maps into $\ell^\infty(T)$. Then $X$ and $Y$ are equal in Borel law if and only if all corresponding marginals are equal in law:

$$
(X(t_1),\ldots,X(t_k))\overset{d}= (Y(t_1),\ldots,Y(t_k))
$$

for every $(t_1,\ldots,t_k)\in T^k$ and every $k=1,2,\ldots$.

**3.** Let $X_\alpha:\Omega_\alpha\to\ell^\infty(T)$ be arbitrary. Then $X_\alpha\rightsquigarrow X$ for a tight limit $X$ if and only if $X_\alpha$ is asymptotically tight and

$$
(X_\alpha(t_1),\ldots,X_\alpha(t_k))
\rightsquigarrow\text{a limit}
$$

for every finite $(t_1,\ldots,t_k)\in T^k$ and every $k=1,2,\ldots$.

If $X_\alpha$ is asymptotically tight and its marginals converge weakly to the marginals

$$
(X(t_1),\ldots,X(t_k))
$$

of a stochastic process $X$, then there is a version of $X$ with uniformly bounded sample paths and

$$
X_\alpha\rightsquigarrow X\qquad\text{in }\ell^\infty(T).
$$

# 5. Finite-Partition Criterion for Asymptotic Tightness - van der Vaart & Wellner §1.5, Theorem 1.5.6, p. 36

## Professor Wrote (verbatim)

**Theorem.** A net $X_\alpha:\Omega_\alpha\to\ell^\infty(T)$ is asymptotically tight if and only if $X_\alpha(t)$ is asymptotically tight in $\mathbb R$ for every $t\in T$ and, for every $\varepsilon>0$ and $\eta>0$, there exists a finite partition

$$
T=\bigcup_{i=1}^kT_i
$$

such that

$$
\limsup_\alpha P^\star\left(
\sup_{1\le i\le k}\sup_{s,t\in T_i}|X_\alpha(s)-X_\alpha(t)|>\varepsilon
\right)<\eta.
$$

The limit superior is with increasing $\alpha$.

# 6. Asymptotic Equicontinuity and Tightness - van der Vaart & Wellner §1.5, Theorem 1.5.7 and Addendum 1.5.8, p. 37

## Professor Wrote (verbatim)

**Def.** Let $\rho$ be a semimetric on $T$. In a semimetric, $\rho(s,t)=0$ need not imply $s=t$.

A net $X_\alpha:\Omega_\alpha\to\ell^\infty(T)$ is asymptotically uniformly $\rho$-equicontinuous in probability if, for every $\varepsilon>0$ and $\eta>0$, there exists $\delta>0$ such that

$$
\limsup_\alpha P^\star\left(
\sup_{\rho(s,t)<\delta}|X_\alpha(s)-X_\alpha(t)|>\varepsilon
\right)<\eta.
$$

"Different: finite partitions vs balls of covers."

**Theorem.** A net $X_\alpha:\Omega_\alpha\to\ell^\infty(T)$ is asymptotically tight if and only if:

1. $X_\alpha(t)$ is asymptotically tight in $\mathbb R$ for every $t\in T$;
2. there exists a semimetric $\rho$ on $T$ such that $(T,\rho)$ is totally bounded and $X_\alpha$ is asymptotically uniformly $\rho$-equicontinuous in probability.

$T$ can be covered by a finite number of $\rho$-balls of radius $\delta$:

$$
\{s:\rho(t,s)<\delta\}.
$$

Moreover, if $X_\alpha\rightsquigarrow X$, then almost all paths $t\mapsto X(t,\omega)$ are uniformly $\rho$-continuous. This $\rho$ can always be taken equal to any semimetric $\rho'$ for which this path-continuity property holds and $(T,\rho')$ is totally bounded.

# 7. Moment Semimetrics - van der Vaart & Wellner §1.5, p. 39

## Professor Wrote (verbatim)

For $0<p<\infty$, consider the family of semimetrics

$$
\rho_p(s,t)=\left(E|X(s)-X(t)|^p\right)^{1/(p\vee1)}.
$$

Here $p\vee1=\max(1,p)$. If $p<1$, the denominator of the exponent is 1: "ignore root."
