# 1. Completion of the Random Entropy CLT Proof - van der Vaart & Wellner §2.11.1, Theorem 2.11.1

## Professor Wrote (verbatim)

We are proving the general CLT for $\{Z_{ni}\}$. We need to show asymptotic equicontinuity.

**(1)** Probability symmetrization lemma.

**(2)** Define

$$
A_n=\left\{
\bigl(Z_{n1}(f)-Z_{n1}(g),\ldots,Z_{nm_n}(f)-Z_{nm_n}(g)\bigr):
(f,g)\in\mathcal F\times\mathcal F,\ \rho(f,g)<\delta_n
\right\}.
$$

The Rademacher process is

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{m_n}\epsilon_i a_i,
\qquad a\in A_n.
$$

Let

$$
\theta_n^2=
\sup_{a\in A_n}\sum_{i=1}^{m_n}a_i^2
=
\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{m_n}a_i^2\right\|_{A_n}.
$$

We need to show $\theta_n\overset{P^\star }{\longrightarrow}0$.

**(3)** Let $B_n$ be an $\varepsilon$-net of $A_n$ in the Euclidean norm on $\mathbb R^{m_n}$. The idea is to use symmetrization to relate $\theta_n^2$ and

$$
\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{m_n}\epsilon_i a_i^2\right\|_{A_n}.
$$

In the preceding lecture we showed

$$
\begin{aligned}
P\left(\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{m_n}\epsilon_i a_i^2\right\|_{A_n}>t\right)
\leq{}&P^\star (|B_n|>M)\\
&+\frac Kt\left[
\varepsilon^2+
\bigl(\varepsilon+\eta_n\sqrt{\log M}\bigr)
E\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{m_n}a_i^2\right\|_{A_n}^{1/2}
\right],
\end{aligned}
$$

where $\|Z_{ni}\|_{\mathcal F}\leq\eta_n\downarrow0$ after truncation and

$$
|B_n|\leq N^2(\varepsilon/2,\mathcal F,d_n)
$$

is bounded in probability. We will choose $M$.

**(4)** Apply the Hoffmann–Jørgensen inequality to

$$
\|S_n\|=\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{m_n}\epsilon_i a_i^2\right\|_{A_n}
$$

with $p=1$.

Choose $M$ such that, for sufficiently large $n$,

$$
P^\star (|B_n|>M)\leq\frac{1-v_1}{2}.
$$

Choose

$$
t_n=\frac{2K}{1-v_1}\left[
\varepsilon^2+
\bigl(\varepsilon+\eta_n\sqrt{\log M}\bigr)
E\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{m_n}a_i^2\right\|_{A_n}^{1/2}
\right].
$$

Then

$$
P(\|S_n\|>t_n)
\leq\frac{1-v_1}{2}+\frac{1-v_1}{2}
=1-v_1,
$$

so $G_n^{-1}(v_1)\leq t_n$, where $G_n^{-1}$ is the quantile function of $\|S_n\|$.

The Hoffmann–Jørgensen inequality yields

$$
\begin{aligned}
E\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{m_n}\epsilon_i a_i^2\right\|_{A_n}
&\lesssim E\left\|\max_{1\leq i\leq m_n}a_i^2\right\|_{A_n}+t_n\\
&\lesssim\eta_n^2+\varepsilon^2+
\bigl(\varepsilon+\eta_n\sqrt{\log M}\bigr)
E\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{m_n}a_i^2\right\|_{A_n}^{1/2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

**(5)** By the expectation symmetrization lemma and the increment second-moment condition,

$$
\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{m_n}Ea_i^2\right\|_{A_n}\longrightarrow0,
$$

and hence, up to constants,

$$
E\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{m_n}a_i^2\right\|_{A_n}
\lesssim
E\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{m_n}\epsilon_i a_i^2\right\|_{A_n}+o(1).
$$

Write

$$
\tau_n=E\left\|\sum_{i=1}^{m_n}a_i^2\right\|_{A_n}.
$$

For an arbitrarily small $\zeta>0$, choose $\varepsilon$ sufficiently small and then $n$ sufficiently large. The preceding bounds and Jensen's inequality give

$$
0\leq\tau_n\leq\zeta+\zeta\sqrt{\tau_n}.
$$

Thus

$$
\tau_n-\zeta\sqrt{\tau_n}-\zeta\leq0,
$$

$$
\sqrt{\tau_n}\leq\frac{\zeta+\sqrt{\zeta^2+4\zeta}}2,
$$

and

$$
\tau_n\leq\frac14\bigl(\zeta+\sqrt{\zeta^2+4\zeta}\bigr)^2.
$$

Letting $\zeta\downarrow0$ gives $\tau_n\longrightarrow0$, and therefore $\theta_n\overset{P^\star }{\longrightarrow}0$. This completes the equicontinuity argument.

# 2. The Iid Empirical Process - van der Vaart & Wellner §2.11.1, Example 2.11.3

## Professor Wrote (verbatim)

**Example 1.** Set

$$
Z_{ni}(f)=\frac1{\sqrt n}f(X_i),
$$

where the $X_i$ are i.i.d.

The theorem says that $\mathcal F$ is Donsker if it is suitably measurable, totally bounded in $L_2(P)$, possesses a square-integrable envelope $F$, and, for every $\delta_n\downarrow0$,

$$
\int_0^{\delta_n}
\sqrt{\log N\bigl(\varepsilon,\mathcal F,L_2(\mathbb P_n)\bigr)}\,d\varepsilon
\overset{P}{\longrightarrow}0.
$$

Recall the Donsker theorem without bracketing, with uniform entropy condition

$$
\int_0^\infty\sup_Q
\sqrt{\log N\bigl(\varepsilon\|F\|_{L_2(Q)},\mathcal F,L_2(Q)\bigr)}\,d\varepsilon
<\infty,
$$

where $Q$ ranges over discrete probability measures.

# 3. Truncated Processes - van der Vaart & Wellner §2.11.1, Example 2.11.4

## Professor Wrote (verbatim)

**Example 2.** Consider $Z_{n1},\ldots,Z_{nm_n}$ such that

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{m_n}P^\star (\|Z_{ni}\|_{\mathcal F}>\eta)
\longrightarrow0
\qquad\text{for every }\eta>0.
$$

Introduce

$$
Z_{ni,\eta}(f)
:=Z_{ni}(f)I(\|Z_{ni}\|_{\mathcal F}\leq\eta).
$$

The truncated processes satisfy

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{m_n}Z_{ni}-\sum_{i=1}^{m_n}Z_{ni,\eta}
\overset{P^\star }{\longrightarrow}0
\quad\text{in }\ell^\infty(\mathcal F),
\qquad\text{for every }\eta>0.
$$

For every $\eta_n\downarrow0$, the processes $Z_{ni,\eta_n}$ satisfy the Lindeberg condition.

If the truncated processes satisfy the remaining conditions of the theorem, then

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{m_n}\bigl(Z_{ni}-EZ_{ni,\eta_n}\bigr)
$$

converges weakly in $\ell^\infty(\mathcal F)$.

# 4. Weighted Empirical Processes - van der Vaart & Wellner §2.11.1.1, Example 2.11.8

## Professor Wrote (verbatim)

**Example 3.** For every $n$, let $X_{n1},\ldots,X_{nm_n}$ be independent random elements in a measurable space $(\mathcal X,\mathcal A)$. Let $X_{ni}$ have law $P_{ni}$, and let $P_{ni}f$ exist for every $f\in\mathcal F$, where $\mathcal F$ is a class of measurable functions $f:\mathcal X\to\mathbb R$.

Given a triangular array of constants $c_{ni}$, define

$$
\mathbb G_n^w(f)
:=\sum_{i=1}^{m_n}c_{ni}\bigl(f(X_{ni})-P_{ni}f\bigr).
$$

Suppose $\mathcal F$ satisfies the uniform entropy condition,

$$
\max_{1\leq i\leq m_n}|c_{ni}|\longrightarrow0,
$$

and

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{m_n}c_{ni}^2P_{ni}\leq P
$$

for a probability measure $P$ with $P^\star F^2<\infty$.

Then $\mathbb G_n^w$ converges weakly to a Gaussian process in $\ell^\infty(\mathcal F)$ provided the sequence converges marginally.

# 5. The Bracketing Central Limit Theorem - van der Vaart & Wellner §2.11.2, Theorem 2.11.9

## Professor Wrote (verbatim)

What about bracketing?

For every $n$, let $Z_{n1},\ldots,Z_{nm_n}$ be independent stochastic processes indexed by $\mathcal F$.

Define $N_{[]}(\varepsilon,\mathcal F,L_2^n)$ as the minimal number $N_\varepsilon$ of sets in a partition

$$
\mathcal F=\bigcup_{j=1}^{N_\varepsilon}\mathcal F_{\varepsilon j}^{n}
$$

such that, for each partitioning set,

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{m_n}E^\star\sup_{f,g\in\mathcal F_{\varepsilon j}^{n}}
|Z_{ni}(f)-Z_{ni}(g)|^2\leq\varepsilon^2.
$$

The partition may depend on $n$.

**Bracketing CLT.** For every $n$, let $Z_{n1},\ldots,Z_{nm_n}$ be independent stochastic processes with finite second moments, indexed by a totally bounded semimetric space $(\mathcal F,\rho)$. Suppose

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{m_n}E^\star\left[
\|Z_{ni}\|_{\mathcal F}I(\|Z_{ni}\|_{\mathcal F}>\eta)
\right]\longrightarrow0
\qquad\text{for every }\eta>0,
$$

$$
\sup_{\rho(f,g)<\delta_n}\sum_{i=1}^{m_n}
E\bigl(Z_{ni}(f)-Z_{ni}(g)\bigr)^2\longrightarrow0
\qquad\text{for every }\delta_n\downarrow0,
$$

and

$$
\int_0^{\delta_n}\sqrt{\log N_{[]}(\varepsilon,\mathcal F,L_2^n)}\,d\varepsilon
\longrightarrow0
\qquad\text{for every }\delta_n\downarrow0.
$$

Then the sequence

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{m_n}(Z_{ni}-EZ_{ni})
$$

is asymptotically tight in $\ell^\infty(\mathcal F)$ and converges weakly provided it converges marginally.

If the partitions can be chosen independent of $n$, the second condition is not needed.
