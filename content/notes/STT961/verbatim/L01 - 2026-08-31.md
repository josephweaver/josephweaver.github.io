# 1. Outer Expectation and Outer Probability (van der Vaart & Wellner, §1.2, p. 6)

## Professor Wrote (verbatim)

### Chapter 1.2. Preliminaries

Let $(\Omega,\mathcal A,P)$ be an arbitrary Prob. space.

$$
T:\Omega\to\overline{\mathbb R}=[-\infty,\infty]
$$

is a map.

**Def.** The outer integral of $T$ with respect to $P$ is

$$
E^\star T
:=
\inf\left\{
EU:
U\ge T,\;
U:\Omega\to\overline{\mathbb R}
\text{ is measurable and }EU\text{ exists}
\right\}.
$$

The outer prob. of an arbitrary set is

$$
P^\star (B)
:=
\inf\left\{
P(A):
A\supset B,\;
A\in\mathcal A
\right\}.
$$

**Remark.** Inner integral

$$
E_\star T=-E^\star (-T).
$$

$$
P_\star (B)=1-P^\star (\Omega-B).
$$

# 2. Measurable Cover Function (Envelope) (van der Vaart & Wellner, Lemma 1.2.1, pp. 6–7)

## Professor Wrote (verbatim)

**Lemma** (about measurable cover function (envelope))

For any map $T:\Omega\to\overline{\mathbb R}$ $\exists$ a measurable map $T^\star :\Omega\to\overline{\mathbb R}$ such that

$$
T^\star\ge T
\quad\text{and}\quad
T^\star\le U\text{ a.s.}
$$

For every measurable $U:\Omega\to\overline{\mathbb R}$ with $U\ge T$ a.s.

Then for any such $T^\star $ we have

$$
E^\star T=ET^\star
$$

provided $ET^\star $ exists.

$$
E^\star T<\infty\quad\Longrightarrow\quad ET^\star\text{ exists}.
$$

**Def.** $T^\star $ is a minimal measurable majorant.

(there is a whole equivalence class of a.s. equal functions)

$$
T_\star =-(-T)^\star
$$

is a maximal measurable minorant.

# 3. Fubini's Theorems — Classical Fubini (van der Vaart & Wellner, §1.2, pp. 10–11 (background to Lemma 1.2.6))

## Professor Wrote (verbatim)

### Fubini's Theorems

Let

$$
T:\Omega_1\times\Omega_2\to\overline{\mathbb R}
$$

be a map on

$$
(\Omega_1\times\Omega_2,\mathcal A_1\times\mathcal A_2,P_1\times P_2).
$$

**Remark:** classical Fubini's Th.

If $f$ is nonnegative and measurable on $A\times B$ (or integrable with respect to the product measure), then

$$
\int_{A\times B}f(x,y)\,dP(x,y)
=
\int_A\left(\int_B f(x,y)\,dP_2(y)\right)dP_1(x).
$$

# 4. Fubini in the Non-Measurable World — Theorem 1 (van der Vaart & Wellner, Lemma 1.2.6, p. 11)

## Professor Wrote (verbatim)

### Fubini in Non-Measurable World

**Th. 1.**

$$
E_\star T
\leq E_{1*}E_{2*}T
\leq E_1^\star E_2^\star T
\leq E^\star T.
$$

Where $\forall \omega_1\in\Omega_1$,

$$
(E_2^\star T)(\omega_1)
=
\inf\left\{
\int U(\omega_2)\,dP_2(\omega_2):
\begin{array}{l}
U:\Omega_2\to\overline{\mathbb R}\text{ meas.}\\
U(\omega_2)\geq T(\omega_1,\omega_2)
\quad\forall\omega_2\\
\text{and }\int U\,dP_2\text{ exists}
\end{array}
\right\}.
$$

$$
E_1^\star\left(\underbrace{E_2^\star T}_{\text{map in }\Omega_1\to\overline{\mathbb R}}\right)
$$

# 5. Fubini in the Non-Measurable World — Proof of Theorem 1 (van der Vaart & Wellner, Lemma 1.2.6, p. 11)

## Professor Wrote (verbatim)

**Proof** only proof

$$
E_1^\star E_2^\star T\leq E^\star T.
$$

Assume $E^\star T<\infty$ by lemma find $T^\star $;

$$
E^\star T=ET^\star
$$

with $(T^\star )^+$ integrable.

$\omega_2\mapsto T^\star (\omega_1,\omega_2)$ is a meas. majorant for $P_1$-a.a. $\omega_1$, of $\omega_2\mapsto T(\omega_1,\omega_2)$.

$$
\int T^\star (\omega_1,\omega_2)\,dP_2(\omega_2)
\geq E_2^\star T(\omega_1)
$$

for a.a. $\omega_1$.

$$
\int T^\star
=
\int(T^\star )^+-\int(T^\star )^-.
$$

### Another theorem: has equal.

**Th. 2** Let $\Omega_1$ be a separable metric space equipped with its Borel $\sigma$-alg. $\mathcal A_1$.

Suppose $T:\Omega_1\times\Omega_2\to\mathbb R$ is defined and

$$
|T(\omega_1,\omega_2)-T(\omega_1',\omega_2)|
\leq
d(\omega_1,\omega_1')H(\omega_2)
$$

for a function $H$ with

$$
E_2^\star H<\infty
$$

and for every sufficiently small $d(\omega_1,\omega_1')$.

Then

$$
E^\star T=E_1E_2^\star T
$$

whenever $E^\star T<\infty$.
