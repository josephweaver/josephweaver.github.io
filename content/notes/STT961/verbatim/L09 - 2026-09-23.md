# 1. The Increment Bound to Be Proved - van der Vaart & Wellner §2.2, Theorem 2.2.4, p. 98

## Professor Wrote (verbatim)

Let $\psi(0)=0$, with $\psi$ convex, nondecreasing, and nonzero, and suppose

$$
\limsup_{x,y\to\infty}\frac{\psi(x)\psi(y)}{\psi(cxy)}<\infty
\quad\text{for some }c>0.
$$

Let $\{X_t:t\in T\}$ be a separable stochastic process satisfying

$$
\|X_s-X_t\|_\psi\leq C d(s,t),\qquad s,t\in T,
$$

where $d$ is a semimetric on $T$.

Then, for every $\eta,\delta>0$,

$$
\left\|\sup_{d(s,t)\leq\delta}|X_s-X_t|\right\|_\psi
\leq K\left[
\int_0^\eta\psi^{-1}\bigl(D(\varepsilon,d)\bigr)\,d\varepsilon
+\delta\psi^{-1}\bigl(D(\eta,d)^2\bigr)
\right].
$$

$K$ depends on $\psi$ and $C$ only.

The integral is labeled “Dudley's integral.”

# 2. Nested Separated Sets and Chains - van der Vaart & Wellner §2.2, proof of Theorem 2.2.4, pp. 98-99

## Professor Wrote (verbatim)

**Proof.** Without loss of generality, assume the packing numbers and the integral are finite; otherwise the upper bound is infinite.

Construct a sieve

$$
T_0\subset T_1\subset T_2\subset\cdots\subset T
$$

such that each $T_j$ is a maximal set with

$$
d(s,t)>\frac\eta{2^j}
\qquad\text{for distinct }s,t\in T_j.
$$

“Maximal”: no point can be added.

[The diagram shows a coarser set of black points and a finer set adding red points, while retaining the black points.]

$$
\#T_j\leq D\left(\frac\eta{2^j},d\right).
$$

Link every $t_{j+1}\in T_{j+1}$ to a unique chosen point $t_j\in T_j$ such that

$$
d(t_j,t_{j+1})\leq\frac\eta{2^j}.
$$

**Question.** Why does such $t_j$ exist?

Suppose no such $t_j$ exists. Then

$$
d(t_j,t_{j+1})>\frac\eta{2^j}
\qquad\text{for every }t_j\in T_j.
$$

We could add $t_{j+1}$ to $T_j$, contradicting maximality.

Now link every $t_{k+1}\in T_{k+1}$ back through

$$
t_{k+1},t_k,t_{k-1},\ldots,t_1,t_0,
\qquad t_0\in T_0.
$$

This is a **chain**; the method is called **chaining**.

# 3. Telescoping and the Covering Integral - van der Vaart & Wellner §2.2, equation (2.2.6), p. 99

## Professor Wrote (verbatim)

Take arbitrary $s_{k+1},t_{k+1}\in T_{k+1}$, with corresponding chains ending at $s_0,t_0$. Then

$$
\begin{aligned}
\left|(X_{s_{k+1}}-X_{s_0})-(X_{t_{k+1}}-X_{t_0})\right|
&=\left|\sum_{j=0}^k(X_{s_{j+1}}-X_{s_j})
-\sum_{j=0}^k(X_{t_{j+1}}-X_{t_j})\right|\\
&\leq2\sum_{j=0}^k\max_{(u,v)\in\mathcal L_j}|X_u-X_v|,
\end{aligned}
$$

where $\mathcal L_j$ consists of the selected links from $T_{j+1}$ to $T_j$.

**Remark.** For every such link,

$$
\|X_u-X_v\|_\psi\leq Cd(u,v)\leq\frac{C\eta}{2^j}.
$$

The number of possible links is at most

$$
\#T_{j+1}\leq D\left(\frac\eta{2^{j+1}},d\right).
$$

Take the maximum over $s,t\in T_{k+1}$ and the Orlicz norm. By the triangle inequality and the finite maximal inequality,

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\left\|\max_{s,t\in T_{k+1}}
\left|(X_s-X_{s_0})-(X_t-X_{t_0})\right|\right\|_\psi\\
&\quad\leq2\sum_{j=0}^k
\left\|\max_{(u,v)\in\mathcal L_j}|X_u-X_v|\right\|_\psi\\
&\quad\leq2K_\psi\sum_{j=0}^k
\psi^{-1}\left(D\left(\frac\eta{2^{j+1}},d\right)\right)
\max_{(u,v)\in\mathcal L_j}\|X_u-X_v\|_\psi\\
&\quad\leq2K_\psi C\sum_{j=0}^k
\psi^{-1}\left(D\left(\frac\eta{2^{j+1}},d\right)\right)\frac\eta{2^j}\\
&\quad\leq K_0\int_0^\eta\psi^{-1}\bigl(D(\varepsilon,d)\bigr)\,d\varepsilon.
\end{aligned}
$$

Here $K_0$ depends only on $\psi$ and $C$.

# 4. Controlling Chain Endpoints - van der Vaart & Wellner §2.2, proof of Theorem 2.2.4, pp. 99-100

## Professor Wrote (verbatim)

For pairs of endpoints $(s_0,t_0)\in T_0^2$ that arise from pairs $s,t\in T_{k+1}$ with $d(s,t)\leq\delta$, choose one representative pair

$$
(u_{s_0,t_0},v_{s_0,t_0})\in T_{k+1}^2
$$

with those endpoints and

$$
d(u_{s_0,t_0},v_{s_0,t_0})\leq\delta.
$$

The number of such endpoint pairs is at most

$$
(\#T_0)^2\leq D(\eta,d)^2.
$$

Telescope again:

$$
\begin{aligned}
|X_{s_0}-X_{t_0}|
&\leq\left|(X_{s_0}-X_{u_{s_0,t_0}})
-(X_{t_0}-X_{v_{s_0,t_0}})\right|\\
&\qquad+|X_{u_{s_0,t_0}}-X_{v_{s_0,t_0}}|.
\end{aligned}
$$

The first term is controlled by the previous chain bound. For arbitrary nearby $s,t\in T_{k+1}$,

$$
X_s-X_t=(X_s-X_{s_0})-(X_t-X_{t_0})+(X_{s_0}-X_{t_0}).
$$

Combining the two decompositions gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
\left\|\max_{\substack{s,t\in T_{k+1}\\d(s,t)\leq\delta}}|X_s-X_t|\right\|_\psi
&\leq2K_0\int_0^\eta\psi^{-1}\bigl(D(\varepsilon,d)\bigr)\,d\varepsilon\\
&\qquad+\left\|\max_{(s_0,t_0)}
|X_{u_{s_0,t_0}}-X_{v_{s_0,t_0}}|\right\|_\psi.
\end{aligned}
$$

The final maximum contains at most $D(\eta,d)^2$ terms. Therefore

$$
\begin{aligned}
\left\|\max_{(s_0,t_0)}
|X_{u_{s_0,t_0}}-X_{v_{s_0,t_0}}|\right\|_\psi
&\leq K_\psi\psi^{-1}\bigl(D(\eta,d)^2\bigr)
\max_{(s_0,t_0)}\|X_{u_{s_0,t_0}}-X_{v_{s_0,t_0}}\|_\psi\\
&\leq K_\psi C\delta\,\psi^{-1}\bigl(D(\eta,d)^2\bigr).
\end{aligned}
$$

Let $k\to\infty$ and combine constants. This gives

$$
\left\|\sup_{d(s,t)\leq\delta}|X_s-X_t|\right\|_\psi
\leq K\left[
\int_0^\eta\psi^{-1}\bigl(D(\varepsilon,d)\bigr)\,d\varepsilon
+\delta\psi^{-1}\bigl(D(\eta,d)^2\bigr)
\right].
$$

# 5. The Corollary and a Bound on the Process - van der Vaart & Wellner §2.2, Corollary 2.2.5 and following discussion, pp. 98, 100

## Professor Wrote (verbatim)

**Proof of corollary.** Take

$$
\eta=\operatorname{diam}T.
$$

Then $T_0=\{t_0\}$, so all chains have the same endpoint. Hence

$$
\left\|\sup_{s,t\in T}|X_s-X_t|\right\|_\psi
\leq K\int_0^{\operatorname{diam}T}
\psi^{-1}\bigl(D(\varepsilon,d)\bigr)\,d\varepsilon.
$$

Alternatively, take $\eta=\delta=\operatorname{diam}T$ in the theorem. Then

$$
D(\eta,d)=1,
$$

and the second term can be absorbed into the integral, with a change in the constant.

**Note.** For every $t_0\in T$,

$$
\left\|\sup_{t\in T}|X_t|\right\|_\psi
\leq\|X_{t_0}\|_\psi
+K\int_0^{\operatorname{diam}T}
\psi^{-1}\bigl(D(\varepsilon,d)\bigr)\,d\varepsilon.
$$
