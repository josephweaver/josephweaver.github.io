# Background Prototype: 1920s Alhazred

An experimental alternative to the paired skill dice in the [August playtest](2026-08-playtest.md). Each check combines one **Background die** with one **Skill die**, including both flat bonuses. Background represents what you grew up around; Skill represents subsequent training and practice. Background stays fixed while Skill can improve.

These are fictional upbringing templates for Alhazred, not claims about everyone from a place, occupation, or community. Choose appearance, identity, and current occupation separately. Magic represents the setting's supernatural fiction.

## Building a Background

Spend **8 background points (BP)** across the eight skills: Athletics, Melee, Ranged, Thievery, Lore, Science, Social, and Magic. Each starts with a **d12 Background die at no cost**. Buy one final rating per skill; costs below are total prices, not cumulative upgrade costs. Unlisted skills remain d12.

| Background die | BP cost |
|---|---:|
| `d12` | 0 |
| `d10 + 2` | 2 |
| `d8 + 4` | 4 |
| `d6 + 6` | 8 |
| `d4 + 8` | 16 (outside the starting budget) |

The examples use three standard distributions, each costing exactly 8 BP:

| Distribution | Allocation | Cost |
|---|---|---:|
| Broad | Four skills at `d10 + 2`; four at d12 | 4 × 2 = 8 |
| Focused | Two skills at `d8 + 4`; six at d12 | 2 × 4 = 8 |
| Specialized | One skill at `d6 + 6`; seven at d12 | 1 × 8 = 8 |

Mixed purchases can also total 8 BP: one `d8 + 4` and two `d10 + 2` ratings cost `4 + 2 + 2 = 8`. The templates below keep the three standard distributions separate for comparison. BP buys Background only; the starting Skill allocation remains a separate prototype decision.

**Starting ceiling:** If the highest starting Skill die is `d8 + 4`, the strongest starting combination is Background `d6 + 6` plus Skill `d8 + 4`: **`d6 + d8 + 10`**, averaging **18**. Under the former 16-BP budget, Background `d4 + 8` could combine with that Skill die for `d4 + d8 + 12`, averaging 19. Background remains fixed after creation; the d4 price is retained only as a reference for alternative budgets.

## Broad Backgrounds

Each background below gives **four listed skills `d10 + 2`**; all others remain d12. **Cost: 8 BP.**

### Traveling Show Household

**Skills:** Athletics, Thievery, Social, Magic.

You grew up moving between towns with a small traveling show. Your childhood included hauling scenery, slipping behind curtains, telling stories, and drawing a crowd. Among the stage tricks, somebody kept a few genuinely dangerous rites.

**Background Leverage examples:** Improvising with stage equipment, reading an audience, recognizing a trick concealed inside a supernatural spectacle.

### Isolated Woodland Household

**Skills:** Athletics, Ranged, Science, Magic.

Your household had to manage a little of everything: timber, hunting, repairs, bargaining, local history, quiet movement through the woods, and defending itself. In Alhazred, the protective customs taught at the hearth sometimes work.

**Background Leverage examples:** Making do far from shops, recognizing familiar woodland hazards, maintaining a household ward.

## Background and Skill Leverage

When an advantage comes from upbringing or familiar circumstances, add a die to the **Background pool**. When it comes from trained technique or an immediate tactical opening, add a die to the **Skill pool**. Keep the highest die in each pool, then add both flat bonuses. Independent advantages can benefit both pools on the same check.

**Lumberjack example:** The Lumber Camp background gives Melee `d8 + 4`. With Skill Melee `d10 + 2`, normally roll `d8 + d10 + 6`. A stumbling monster grants Skill Leverage: keep the higher of `2d10`. If that monster is made of wood, knowledge of splitting timber grants Background Leverage: keep the higher of `2d8`. With both, roll `highest(2d8) + highest(2d10) + 6`. A custom lumberjack could instead spend all 8 BP on Melee `d6 + 6`, leaving every other Background die at d12.

Any die rolled showing its maximum is a Partial Critical trigger under the current playtest wording; resolve the check against its DC as usual. Background Leverage requires a relevant connection to the situation, not merely possessing a background rating.

## Questions for the Prototype

- Do broad backgrounds get enough use from their four modest ratings?
- Is one specialized rating worth giving up improvements to other skills?
- Does fixed Background feel like meaningful history or an unwanted ceiling on later development?
- Is assigning Leverage to Background or Skill quick and consistent at the table?
- How should starting Skill dice be allocated, and how should repeated Leverage sources stack?

Record check success rates, Partial Critical triggers, and how often each background earns Leverage. Equal BP budgets are a design hypothesis, not a claim that the three distributions are equally powerful.
