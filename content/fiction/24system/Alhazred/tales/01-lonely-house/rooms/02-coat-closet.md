## Room 2: Coat Closet

### Read Aloud

> The small door opens into a surprisingly deep closet. Several coats hang from wooden pegs along the walls. Some are practical travelling coats. Others appear more expensive, suitable for business or social occasions. A woman's rain cloak hangs among them. Below rest pairs of shoes, carefully arranged in rows. Mud still clings faintly to some of the soles. Several umbrellas lean in a corner beside a pair of snowshoes and a weathered walking stick. The air smells faintly of cedar and old wool. Though everything appears orderly, a strange impression lingers. The collection feels less like one person's belongings and more like the accumulation of many visitors over many years.

---

### The Rain Cloak

Read Aloud

Among the heavier coats hangs a woman's rain cloak of dark green canvas. Though plainly made, it has been carefully maintained. The cuffs show signs of repeated repair, and one pocket has been restitched by a skilled hand.

If a player takes this coat the become associated with **Ms.J**.

### The Traveling Coat

Read Aloud

Hanging apart from the others is a tailored woman's traveling coat of charcoal wool. The cut is modern and fashionable without being extravagant. Brass buttons run neatly down the front, and the interior lining bears the stitched initials: `E.W.`.

If a player takes this coat they beomce associated with **The Business Partner**.
