## Room 18: The Attic

### At-a-glance

- **Features:** Dusty trunks, stored furniture, a fallen cabinet.
- **Discovery:** Agatha Pembroke’s remains pinned beneath the cabinet.
- **Secret:** The cabinet blocks a concealed entrance to the artifact chamber.
- **Encounter:** Moving the cabinet causes the House to recognize and animate Agatha.

### Read Aloud

> Dust blankets the trunks, rolled carpets, and furniture crowded beneath the sloping roof. Narrow paths wind between them.
>
> At the far end, a tall cabinet lies tipped against the wall. A skeletal hand protrudes beneath it, a tarnished ring still encircling one finger.
>
> Beyond the fingertips, deep scratches mark a narrow seam in the paneling.

### Agatha’s Remains

Agatha discovered the concealed entrance while searching for the family fortune. While moving the cabinet, she tipped it onto herself and became trapped. She eventually died within reach of the door.

Her brittle bones remain tangled in an elderly woman’s clothing. A small purse contains loose coins, several keys, and a handkerchief embroidered **A.P.**

The scratches around the panel mark her attempts to reach the entrance and free herself.

**Her remains do not move or animate while the cabinet is in place.**

### The Blocked Entrance

The cabinet is propped against the secret panel. **It must be moved before the entrance to the artifact chamber can be opened.**

Moving it exposes Agatha’s remains fully, allowing the House to recognize her at last.

### Read Aloud — The House Finds Agatha

> As the cabinet moves away, the bones beneath it settle with a faint clatter.
>
> A whisper passes through the attic.
>
> “Agatha? There you are.”
>
> A pale likeness gathers around the remains: an elderly woman in a high-collared dress.
>
> “Look. You were so close.”
>
> The bones rise from the floor, settling into their places within her translucent body. One hand reaches toward the seam in the wall.
>
> “Go on. Say, ‘It must be here somewhere.’”
>
> Her jaw opens. A dry, harsh rasp answers:
>
> “…here somewhere…”

### The Treasure Is Hers

Agatha initially ignores the players and resumes searching for a way to open the panel.

If someone reaches for the entrance or interferes with her search, she turns toward them.

> “Mine.”
>
> Her fingers spread across the panel.
>
> “You never believed me. None of you.”
>
> The House whispers, “Show them, Agatha.”

She attacks anyone she believes intends to steal her discovery. Stepping away and acknowledging her claim may buy time, but she remains suspicious.

### Combat

Use the **Spectral Skeleton** stat block.

Agatha guards the entrance while continuing her search. When possible, one hand works at the panel while the other attacks or grasps an intruder.

Destroying her torso allows the surviving head and limbs to move independently, without granting additional actions. Her skull floats before the entrance, rasping “Mine,” while her scattered limbs continue searching and defending her claim.

The House encourages her throughout:

> “That’s it, Agatha. Just a little farther.”

### The Concealed Door

With the cabinet removed, the secret panel can be opened to reveal the entrance to the artifact chamber.

Agatha found the entrance. She never discovered what lay beyond it.

### The Listener

Resting within the crate is an object roughly the size of a large melon. Its shape resembles an egg or seed fashioned from pale translucent stone. The surface consists of countless loops and channels fused into a single lattice. Some pass through one another impossibly. Others emerge from the stone only to disappear back within it. The entire structure resembles blown glass that has somehow fossilized.

At the apex of the object is a single smooth opening approximately two inches across. Unlike the rest of the artifact, the opening is perfectly circular. Deliberate. Worn. As though countless hands have touched its edge over a very long time.

Looking into the aperture reveals no obvious interior cavity. Instead, a translucent passage curves gently downward before bending out of sight. Following its course proves strangely difficult. The tunnel appears to twist through the object at impossible angles, occasionally vanishing behind surfaces that should not obstruct the view. More than one observer becomes convinced the passage is larger inside than the object itself.
