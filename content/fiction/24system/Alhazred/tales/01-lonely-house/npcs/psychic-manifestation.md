### Psychic Manifestation

**Stamina:** 3
**Health:** R: 3, L: 3, H: 3, F: 4, T: 5.

**Special — Incorporeal:** The manifestation can pass through solid objects, walls, and doors. Physical attacks cannot harm it while incorporeal.

**Special — Manifest:** The creature can become corporeal to physically interact with reality. It remains corporeal until the start of its next turn. While corporeal, physical attacks affect it normally.

**Special — Salt:** Exposure to salt forces the creature to manifest, including attempting to step over a salt line. It remains corporeal until the start of its next turn, or longer if exposure continues.

**Description:** A spectral creature given form by psychic activity. It drifts through solid matter, becoming tangible when it reaches out to affect the physical world.
