## Monsters

### Goblet

**Skills:** Athletics 1, Melee 1
**Armor:** 0
**Stamina:** N/A
**Fear:** N/A
**Health:** Head 1, Torso 1, Legs 1

**Actions (H)**
**Bite:** 1 damage.
**Eat Food:** Consumes a significant portion of food. As it feeds, the Goblet undergoes a rapid mitosis-like division, spawning one new Goblet.

**Special:** Goblets are sticky and may climb walls and ceilings without using limbs. Though bipedal, Goblets have no arms.

**Description:** A Goblet stands roughly one foot tall, its pale body resembling a lump of spoiled dough sagging beneath its own weight. It walks upon two stubby legs but possesses no arms. Most of its upper body consists of an oversized gullet capable of swallowing objects seemingly too large to fit inside it. Beyond the mouth is no ordinary throat, only a writhing mass of weevils forming a living funnel into its body.

Goblets are voracious scavengers, particularly attracted to flour, bread, and provisions carried by travelers. As a Goblet feeds, its dough-like body swells and begins developing a deep crease down its center. The mass gradually pulls apart until two smaller Goblets tear free of one another, each immediately searching for more food.
