### Spectral Skeleton

**Skills:** Melee 1
**Armor:** 0 · **Stamina:** n/a · **Fear:** n/a
**Health:** Head 2, Torso 3, Each Arm 2, Legs 3.

**Actions (RLHF):**

- **Bite (Head):** 1 damage.
- **Claw / Grasp (Arm):** 1 damage or grab.
- **Kick (Leg):** 1 damage.

**Special — Suspended Bones:** Bones float within a spectral image of their former owner. Destroying the torso allows surviving parts to fly apart and move independently, each carrying its portion of the spectral image. Each part retains only its associated actions; separation grants no additional actions.

**Special — Bone Destruction:** Filling a hit location destroys its bones and removes its actions. The skeleton remains active until every location is destroyed.

**Description:** Brittle bones suspended within a translucent likeness of the person they once belonged to. When broken apart, the scattered remains continue acting beneath fragments of their former face and flesh.
